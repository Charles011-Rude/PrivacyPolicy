## 1. Overview
In compliance with **Google Play's User Data policy on Account Deletion**, Colette's Food Inc. provides a transparent and accessible mechanism for all users to request the deletion of their accounts and associated personal data.

Whether you are an active employee, manager, delivery rider, cashier, or general user, you have the right to request full account removal without needing to retain the mobile application on your device.

---

## 2. How to Request Account and Data Deletion

You can request account deletion through any of the following methods:

### Method A: Via Direct Email (Recommended for Play Store compliance)
1. Send an email to **charlespajutan011@gmail.com** from the email address linked to your Colette's Food Inc. account.
2. Use the subject line: **`Account and Data Deletion Request`**
3. In the body of your message, please provide:
   - Your Full Name
   - Your Registered Account Email
   - Your Branch / Store Assignment (if applicable)
   - Reason for deletion (optional)

### Method B: In-App Request (When logged in)
1. Open the **Colette's Food Inc.** application.
2. Go to **Settings > Account**.
3. Select **Request Account Deletion** and confirm.

---

## 3. What Data Is Deleted Upon Request
Once your identity is confirmed and the request is processed, the following personal data will be permanently purged or anonymized:

- **Authentication Data:** Firebase Authentication credentials, encrypted passwords, session tokens, and security identifiers.
- **Profile Information:** Full name, phone number, profile photo/avatar, and branch associations.
- **Device & Notification Identifiers:** Push notification tokens (FCM tokens) and unique device mapping IDs.
- **Location History:** Any cached or stored GPS coordinates related to rider transit.

---

## 4. What Data Is Retained and Why
Certain operational information must be retained pursuant to applicable taxation, legal, and financial auditing regulations:

- **Sales & Point of Sale (POS) Records:** Historic sales records, tax receipts, and payment transactions cannot be deleted because Philippine business and taxation standards mandate audit trails for official business transactions. However, these records are decoupled and anonymized from your personal profile.
- **Inventory Audit Logs:** Historical stock movements and audit records generated during your employment or session remain part of the branch's operational history for fraud prevention and inventory accuracy.

---

## 5. Processing Timeline
- **Acknowledgment:** Deletion requests submitted via email will be acknowledged within **48 hours**.
- **Completion:** All eligible personal data will be permanently removed from active Firebase databases within **30 calendar days** of verification.
- **Backups:** Routine disaster-recovery database backups containing encrypted snapshots are automatically overwritten and purged in accordance with our routine cloud backup cycle (within 60 days).
