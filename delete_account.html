Account & Data Deletion

How to request the removal of your account and personal data from our systems

Effective Date September 22, 2026
Application Colette's Food Inc.
Package ID com.colettesfoodinc.app
Compliance Google Play Data Safety

1. Overview

In compliance with Google Play's User Data & Account Deletion Policy, Colette's Food Inc. provides a simple, direct mechanism for all users to request the permanent deletion of their account and associated personal information.

You have the right to request account and personal data deletion at any time without needing to keep the mobile application installed on your device.

2. How to Submit a Deletion Request

You can request account deletion via our official administrator email or directly from the application:

Request Deletion via Email (Recommended)

Click the button below to submit a pre-formatted deletion request from your registered email address, or send an email directly to charlespajutan011@gmail.com.

✉ Submit Deletion Request by Email

Email Request Requirements

When sending your email request, please make sure to include:

  1. Subject: Account and Data Deletion Request
  2. Registered Email: The exact email used to register or log in to the Colette's Food Inc. app.
  3. Full Name: Your registered full name.
  4. Branch Assignment: Store or branch location (if applicable).

3. Data Treatment Upon Deletion

The table below details what happens to each category of data once your request is processed:

Data Type Status Description & Details
Account Credentials Deleted Firebase Authentication records, password hashes, and login access tokens are permanently purged.
Personal Profile Deleted Name, phone number, avatar/profile photos, and employee/user ID mapping are permanently deleted.
Device Identifiers & FCM Deleted Push notification tokens and device linkage are removed from active databases.
Location Data Deleted Any historical coordinates, rider transit cache, or tracking session data are wiped.
Sales & POS Records Retained (Anonymized) Historic sales receipts and transaction figures are retained in anonymized form strictly for mandatory taxation, accounting, and legal audit compliance under Philippine law.
Inventory Audit Logs Retained System-level stock movements and transfer histories remain in company records for branch audit integrity without referencing personal identifiers.

4. Processing Timeline

  • Confirmation: We acknowledge deletion requests within 48 hours of receipt.
  • Execution: All personal account data will be permanently removed from our active production database within 30 calendar days.
  • Backup Overwrite: Encrypted system disaster-recovery backups are purged and overwritten following standard cloud backup retention cycles (up to 60 days).
