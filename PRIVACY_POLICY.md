## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Personal Information
When you register, log in, or use the Application, we may collect personally identifiable information that you voluntarily provide to us, including:
- **Contact & Account Information:** Name, email address, phone number, password (securely hashed), user role (e.g., Administrator, Cashier, Rider, Warehouse Staff), and branch/store assignment.
- **Profile Data:** Profile photo or avatar (optional).

### B. Business & Operational Data
Because the Application serves as an enterprise business management system, we collect operational data necessary for day-to-day operations:
- **Transaction Records:** Sales, Point-of-Sale (POS) transactions, order details, payment methods, and invoices/receipts.
- **Inventory & Supply Chain Data:** Stock counts, transfer requests, waste logs, and product catalog details.
- **Activity & Audit Logs:** System events, timestamps, and user actions for audit and security purposes.

### C. Device & Media Permissions
Depending on the features you use, the Application may request access to:
- **Camera & Photo Gallery:** To capture or upload product images, store receipts, inventory proofs, or delivery documentation. Images are only accessed when explicitly selected or captured by you.
- **Internet & Network State:** Required to communicate with our cloud infrastructure, synchronize inventory, and process transactions in real time.

### D. Location Data
- **Delivery & Route Optimization:** If you are a designated delivery rider or utilizing map navigation features, the Application may access your device's geographical location (coarse or fine) to display delivery routes, optimize transit, and calculate distances via Google Maps.
- We do not sell or trade your location data to any third-party advertisers.

### E. Device & Usage Information
We may automatically collect certain diagnostic and technical information, including:
- Device model, manufacturer, and operating system version.
- Unique device identifiers, IP address, and browser type (for web platform).
- App crash reports, performance metrics, and system diagnostic logs.

We use the collected information for specific, legitimate business purposes, including:

## 5. Data Security
We prioritize the protection of your personal and business information. We implement technical and administrative security measures, including:
- **Encryption:** All communications between the Application and cloud servers are encrypted in transit using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).
- **Access Restrictions:** Role-based security rules enforce strict boundaries so that users only access data relevant to their role and branch.
- **Secure Password Storage:** Passwords are never stored in plain text and are managed using Firebase Authentication's industry-standard cryptographic hashing.

While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your information, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.
