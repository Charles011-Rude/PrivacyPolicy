1. Introduction

Welcome to Colette's Food Inc. ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal data. This Privacy Policy outlines our standards, practices, and procedures regarding the collection, processing, storage, and protection of information obtained through our mobile application, web application, and management portal (collectively, the "Application").

By downloading, installing, registering with, or using the Application, you agree to the collection and use of your information in compliance with this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information necessary to provide efficient, reliable business operations, order processing, inventory control, and delivery services.

A. Personal Information

  • Account Credentials: Full name, verified email address, encrypted authentication credentials, phone number, and store/branch assignment.
  • User Role & Permissions: Assigned system role (e.g., Administrator, Branch Manager, Cashier, Delivery Rider, Warehouse Staff).
  • Profile Assets: Optional user avatar or profile image uploaded for in-app identification.

B. Operational & Transactional Data

  • Sales & POS Records: Order itemization, quantities, totals, payment statuses, discounts, and transaction timestamps.
  • Inventory & Supply Chain: Stock counts, stock transfer requests, batch movements, spoilage/waste entries, and item catalogs.
  • Activity & Audit Trails: Timestamped action logs of transactions, inventory adjustments, and status modifications for corporate accountability.

C. Device Hardware & Media Permissions

  • Camera & Photo Gallery: Accessed strictly when you choose to take or upload photos for product catalogs, receipts, inventory condition proof, or delivery confirmations.
  • Network Status & Internet: Required to establish encrypted connections to our cloud database and synchronize data in real-time.

D. Location Information

For personnel utilizing rider delivery features or map visualization tools, the Application may access your device's geographical location (coarse or fine) to display routes, determine distances, and assist in navigating deliveries via Google Maps Platform. We never sell, rent, or trade your location data to advertisers or third parties.

3. How We Use Collected Data

We process collected data exclusively for legitimate enterprise purposes:

  • Role-Based Access: Authenticating staff members and restricting operational features according to authorized roles.
  • Order & Inventory Fulfillment: Creating, routing, fulfilling, and archiving store transactions and inventory transfers.
  • Delivery Optimization: Calculating transit distances, generating route directions, and assisting riders in timely fulfillment.
  • Audit & Security: Preventing unauthorized activity, maintaining financial integrity, and safeguarding store assets.
  • Application Maintenance: Diagnosing crashes, monitoring app uptime, and optimizing performance.

5. Security of Your Information

The security of your data is paramount. We enforce technical and procedural safeguards, including:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between the app and backend services use TLS 1.3 encryption (HTTPS).
  • Granular Authorization: Cloud Firestore security rules ensure users cannot view or manipulate data beyond their permission tier.
  • Password Hashing: Passwords are never stored in clear text and are processed using secure cryptographic hashes via Firebase Authentication.
